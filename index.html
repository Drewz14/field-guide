FAQ

Is this a physical book?

No. It's a digital PDF you download right away. Print it at home or keep it on your phone.

What size is it?

24 US letter pages (8.5 x 11 in) with large type, so it's readable on a phone screen too.

Is it for beginners?

Yes. It starts with the Golden Rules and focuses on the distinctive species beginners learn first, alongside the lookalikes to avoid.

Can I rely on it alone to identify mushrooms?

No. No guide can replace in-person identification by an expert. Use several sources, learn from local experts, and if you are not 100% sure, don't eat it.

What region does it cover?

The species are common across much of North America and Europe. Seasons vary by region and year.
